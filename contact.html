Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries about our products and services.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Our minimum order quantity varies by product type. For standard designs, we typically require a minimum of 500 yards per design. For custom designs, minimums may be higher. Contact us for specific requirements.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We export to over 50 countries worldwide. We work with reliable shipping partners to ensure your order arrives safely and on time. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by destination.

Can you create custom designs?

Absolutely! Our in-house design team specializes in creating custom patterns. We can work from your concepts or create original designs tailored to your brand. Custom orders typically require 4-6 weeks lead time.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, letters of credit (L/C), and other standard international payment methods. Specific terms are discussed during the ordering process.

Can I visit your facility?

Yes, we welcome visits from potential partners and clients. Please contact us in advance to schedule a factory tour, and we'll be happy to show you our operations.
